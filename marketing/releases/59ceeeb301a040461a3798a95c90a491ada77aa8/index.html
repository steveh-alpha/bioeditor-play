A few things to know.

Is BioEditor free to use?

Yes. This browser alpha is free to use, with no account required and no watermarks on exports.

Where are my figures saved?

Figures are saved in your browser’s local storage on this device. They do not sync across devices. Use “Save file” in the editor to download a backup, especially before clearing browser data.

Can I use a figure in a publication?

You can export SVG and PNG files for your publication workflow. Original BioEditor artwork uses CC BY 4.0 and requires attribution; the editor provides credit text. Check your journal’s requirements and review the science, labels, and final export before submitting.

Is the illustration library scientifically reviewed?

Not yet. BioEditor is an alpha, and its illustrations are conceptual schematics awaiting scientific review. Check that each illustration and template is appropriate for your research.
