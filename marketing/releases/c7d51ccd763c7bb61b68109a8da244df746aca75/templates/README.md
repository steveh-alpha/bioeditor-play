# Diagram templates

`diagrams.js` defines original starter templates and a local composer for process, cycle, branching, and comparison diagrams. The legacy template IDs remain in `core.js`. Figures use native editable objects and attached relationships; template attribution records their declared composition/version. See `plan/biology-coverage.md` for coverage goals and review gates.

`antibodies.js` supplies eight source-linked antibody diagrams, including comparisons with 9–10 items and an anatomy diagram with native editable leaders. This catalog-specific layout does not change the general composer’s 2–8-item limit.

`therapeutics.js` supplies seven populated mechanism/architecture templates, including both ex vivo/in vivo CAR-T delivery paths and RIPTAC hold-and-kill with a TP-negative comparator. Compositions use native objects, text, panels and attached relationships with source-linked draft evidence.

`viruses.js` supplies five populated Virus & VLP templates: particle gallery, AAV gene-therapy MoA, VLP vaccine MoA, empty/partial/full capsid QC and LV/AAV/VLP comparison. Existing support and antibody assets are composed as editable native objects; source evidence and scientific limitations travel with the figure.
